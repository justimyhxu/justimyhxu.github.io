Biography

Yinghao Xu is a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Multimedia Lab (MMLab), Department of Information Engineering in The Chinese University of Hong Kong. His supervisor is Prof. Dahua Lin and he worked closely with Prof. Bolei Zhou. He is very interested in generative models for visual understanding and content recreation, particularly in 3D generative models. He also collaborates with Dr. Bo Dai and Dr. Yujun Shen and Dr. Ceyuan Yang.

Before that, he graduated from Zhejiang University in 2019, advised by Dr. Lechao Cheng and Prof. Chunguang Li.
