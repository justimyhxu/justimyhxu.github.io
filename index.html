Biography

I am a postdoc at Stanford Computational Imaging Lab, Stanford University working with Prof. Gordon Wetzstein. I obtained my Ph.D. degree at Multimedia Lab (MMLab), in The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Bolei Zhou and Prof. Dahua Lin. I am very interested in generative models and 3D vision, particularly in 3D generative models.

During my Ph.D., I collaborate closely with Dr. Yujun Shen, Dr. Ceyuan Yang, Prof. Sida Peng and Dr. Zifan Shi. I had the pleasure to visit Stanford Computational Imaging Lab in 2023, under the guidance of Prof. Gordon Wetzstein.

Previously, I graduated from Zhejiang University in 2019, advised by Dr. Lechao Cheng and Prof. Chunguang Li. In 2018, I was a research assistant at UC San Diego, working with Prof. Hao Su.
